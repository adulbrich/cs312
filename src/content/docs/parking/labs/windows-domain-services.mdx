You've been promoted to Supreme Chancellor of your own (very small) corporate empire. Your first decree: bring order to the chaos. You'll spin up a shiny new Windows Server and install the magical tome of power: Active Directory. You will create digital fiefdoms (Organizational Units), populate them with loyal subjects (user accounts), and issue imperial edicts (Group Policies) to control their desktops. It's good to be the king.
