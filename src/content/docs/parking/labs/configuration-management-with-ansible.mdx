Your Terraform spell created a server, but it's just an empty shell. Now you need to give it a personality. Enter Ansible, your robot butler factory. You'll write a 'playbook' (a set of instructions) that tells your Ansible bots exactly how to configure your servers. 'Go install Nginx. Go deploy this website. And make sure the service is running. Chop chop!' The beauty is, your robot army will do it the exact same way every single time, on one server or a thousand.
